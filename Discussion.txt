5. Integrating llama.cpp with SQLite/ChromaDB
    Since you’re building a "long-term memory" system, you can use llama.cpp for two different things simultaneously:

    The Conversationalist: To talk to the user.

    The Librarian: To turn messages into "embeddings" to save in your database.

    The Workflow:

    User says something.

    Use llm.create_embedding("user text") to get a list of numbers.

    Search SQLite/ChromaDB for past messages with similar numbers.

    Inject those past memories into the prompt so the AI remembers the user's history.  

    Generate the final emotional response.
